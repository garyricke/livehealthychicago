Hi Viviana and Katherine,

Great to hear about the AMA modules. Here’s where the portal stands today and what each piece would take.

1. Housing the modules in the portal

Yes. The portal already hosts training this way: the CHW training lives there as a video page with its materials. CME modules (video, slides or PDFs) could be added the same way, each with its own description and “Start module” button.

2. Secure login, testing and claiming credit

  • Login: not yet. Partners currently share one password. That keeps casual visitors out, but it can’t tell who a learner is, and CME needs to. Individual accounts are possible because the portal already runs on a database that supports them, but that would be new work.
  • Tests and assessments: not yet. They can be built (questions, a passing score and saved results), but they’d also be new work.
  • Claiming credit: adding a “Claim your CME credit” link after each module is easy. The credit itself has to come from the accredited provider, though, and providers usually need the post-test, evaluation and completion records to come from their own system.

My recommendation is to let the accredited provider’s learning platform handle the login, tests and credit, since that’s what accreditation is built around. The portal would then be the front door: it lists the modules for our partners and sends each learner to the right place. That’s quick to set up and avoids building records we’d have to keep audit-ready. If the provider doesn’t have a platform, building it into the portal is possible, and I’d scope it once we know the requirements.

  4. Roughly how many learners do you expect, and when would you like this live?
