2. Ella’s CHW training project — scope + recording (Ella joins)

Ella — I’ve read your script, and it’s genuinely good. The analogies do a lot of work (the garden hose, the slow leak behind the wall), the nutrition section respects people’s food and culture instead of lecturing them, and it’s AHA-cited throughout. The interview structure means it will sound like a conversation, not a lecture. Nice work.

For everyone else, here’s the shape of it — five modules, about 32 minutes:

  • 1. Understanding High Blood Pressure — 6 min
  • 2. Preventing & Managing (nutrition, movement, stress, sleep, medications) — 9 min
  • 3. Know Your Numbers (the two numbers, AHA categories, how to measure correctly) — 8–9 min
  • 4. Recognizing an Emergency (over 180/120, BE FAST) — 4–5 min
  • 5. The CHW’s Role (listening, teach-back, one realistic goal) — 4–5 min

That structure is a gift, actually — five modules become five chapters in the same format as the Nuna interview, and each one yields a short, headlined clip a CHW can watch on the bus before a home visit.
