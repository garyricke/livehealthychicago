Great to meet you too, and thanks for the concept — it's a clear brief. The shape is right for an ad landing page: one promise up top, a "What's Your Why, Chi?" moment, the six everyday tips, and three doors out. Most of those doors already exist on the site, so this is more assembly than invention.

How I'd build it

  • Its own short URL, separate from the homepage, so the ads have a clean link and we can see exactly how many people arrive from the campaign versus everywhere else.
  • Built for a phone first. Nearly everyone who taps an ad will be on one. The six tips become a swipeable row, the three doors stack.
  • Real people in the photo. The image in the concept is a stand-in — I'd use one of the field photos already approved for the site, so the page matches the ads' neighborhoods.
  • The three doors: "Find a blood pressure check" goes to the live events and mobile-team schedule; "Get support" goes to the resources. "Find care near you" is the one we don't have yet — see below.

What I need to know

  • Timing. When do the ads start running, and for how long? That sets my deadline and how long the page should stay up as-is.
  • "Find care near you." What should be behind it — a list of coalition clinics, a CDPH locator, something else? If there's a partner list for the six pilot zip codes, that would do it.
  • Spanish. The pilot spans languages — will there be Spanish-language ads, and if so should the page have a Spanish version on day one?
  • Tracking. Does your team have UTM tags or a pixel you'll want on the page, so the ad platform and the site agree on the numbers?

Send me what you have and I'll get a first version up for the group to click through. Looking forward to it.
