Six small things you can do today

You don’t have to change everything at once. Pick one. Then pick another next week.

01

Eat more fruits and vegetables

They help keep your blood pressure in a healthy range.

02

Move more

Aim for 30 minutes most days. A walk counts — it adds up.

03

Use less salt

Try herbs and spices instead. Less salt can lower your blood pressure.

04

Keep a healthy weight

Even a small amount of weight loss can make a big difference.

05

Get enough sleep

Most adults need 7 to 9 hours a night.

06

Lower your stress

Take time for the things that help you feel your best.
