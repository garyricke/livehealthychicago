Communications & Outreach Subcommittee
“What’s Your Why, Chi?” Pilot
The Live Healthy Chicago “What’s Your Why, Chi?” Pilot has officially launched. Over the past several months, the Communications and Outreach subcommittee, led by the Chicago Department of Public Health, worked together with the University of Chicago Medicine, American Medical Association, American Heart Association, Rush University, and the LHC Community Board to review creative concepts, refine key messages, and develop digital campaign materials that will be tested across six priority Chicago neighborhoods (zip codes 60649, 60637, 60621, 60624, 60623, 60651).

The six-week pilot aims to identify which health messages resonate most with residents across diverse communities, languages, and audiences. Insights gathered through the campaign will help shape future coalition communications, strengthen partner engagement efforts, and inform broader citywide health awareness initiatives.

For patients and health care providers
Help your audience understand blood pressure control, why it matters, and how to take practical steps with their health care professional. Use these messages and creative resources to promote healthier blood pressure and reduce the risk of heart attack, stroke, kidney damage, and dementia.
