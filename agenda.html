Decide todayCME training modules on the portal

Katherine asked whether the portal can host the AMA modules with logins, tests and credit. Here’s what we know.

  • The facts so far. Rush’s CME Office would be the accredited provider. Rush has a learning platform, but it costs $25 per learner. The modules are videos and slides, with 100+ learners per module expected, and possibly more as the program grows.
  • Option A: Rush’s platform, with the portal as the front door. The portal lists the modules and sends partners to Rush’s platform for the login, post-test and credit. It’s quick to set up, and the CME Office handles accreditation. At 100 learners, it costs about $2,500 per module in fees.
  • Option B: build it into the portal. This adds individual logins, a post-test with a passing score, the evaluation, and completion records the CME Office can use to issue credit. There’s no per-learner fee, but it’s a one-time build plus upkeep, and it’s new scope beyond the maintenance package. It only works if the CME Office will accept completion records from a system outside theirs.
The question that decides it
Will Rush’s CME Office issue credit from the portal’s records? And can the $25 be reduced or waived for an internal Rush program? Katherine, could you ask the CME Office? If the answer is yes, I’ll send an estimate for Option B so you can compare the two side by side.
