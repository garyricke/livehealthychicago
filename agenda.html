Demo & decideThe newsletter sender

Built and tested this morning. I’ll share my screen and walk through a send.

  • How it works. Sign in at livehealthychi.com/send with the admin login. Paste in addresses (any format) or add every portal sign-up with one click. Pick the issue, preview it, and send yourself a test. Sending to the list is blocked until that exact version has been tested, and nobody gets the same issue twice.
  • Unsubscribes are handled. Every email has a personal unsubscribe link, and Gmail and Outlook show their own unsubscribe button. Once someone opts out, adding their address again won’t put them back on the list.
